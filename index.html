FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this report card generator completely free? ▼
Yes, 100% free. No registration, no watermarks, no hidden fees. All 16 report card templates and 20 student card designs are free to use forever.
Do I need to register or create an account? ▼
No account needed. Open the app, fill in your details, and download or print. Your data stays only in your browser — we don't save anything to any server.
How do I add my own school name and logo? ▼
In the app sidebar, type your school name in the "School Name" field. It updates live on the template. You can also customize the logo initials in Settings.
Can I add or remove subjects from the report card? ▼
Yes. Click "Add Subject" to add a new row, type the subject name, full marks and obtained marks. Click ✕ to remove any subject. You can have unlimited subjects.
How does the student card expiry work? ▼
Enter the student's class number. If class is 9 or above, the card expires in 2 years from issue date. If below class 9, it expires in 1 year. The expiry date is calculated and printed automatically on the card back.
How to print the report card or student ID card? ▼
Click the 🖨 Print button in the top-right of the app. A print window opens. Choose your printer or select "Save as PDF" to download. Student cards are sized exactly like a credit card.
Does it work on mobile phones? ▼
Yes. The app works on all modern mobile browsers. You can also install it as a PWA (Progressive Web App) — tap Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or install from Chrome on Android.
